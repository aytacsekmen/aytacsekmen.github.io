Aytaç Sekmen

M.Sc. student in Computer Engineering · Middle East Technical University (METU), Ankara

I work on 3D perception for robots and autonomous systems. My research is done at METU ROMER, the Center for Robotics and Artificial Intelligence, with Prof. Sinan Kalkan and Prof. Gökberk Cinbiş.

I started by asking why depth models fail on the Moon, and this led me to study 3D geometric foundation models, how to evaluate them well, and how perception feeds into robot actions through Vision-Language-Action models. My current work is on event cameras: sensors that keep working under extreme lighting and fast motion. In the long run, I want to build embodied AI systems whose perception is reliable enough to act on outside the lab.

Research interests

3D perception

Monocular depth and 3D geometric foundation models, and benchmarks that show why and where they fail.

Embodied AI

How visual representations shape robot policies, and how Vision-Language-Action models generalize.

Robust sensing

Event-based vision and foundation models for perception in harsh conditions, from planetary surfaces to fast motion.

LuMon: A Comprehensive Benchmark and Development Suite with Novel Datasets for Lunar Monocular Depth Estimation
Aytaç Sekmen*, Fatih Emre Gunes*, Furkan Horoz*, Hüseyin Umut Işık*, Mehmet Alp Ozaydin*, Onur Altay Topaloglu*, Şahin Umutcan Üstündaş*, Yurdasen Alp Yeni*, Halil Ersin Soken, Erol Sahin, Ramazan Gokberk Cinbis, Sinan Kalkan
CVPR 2026 Workshops (AI4Space) · *equal contribution
A benchmark for monocular depth estimation on the Moon, with new stereo ground truth from the Chang'e-3 mission and the CHERI dark-analog dataset. Evaluates 14 models across synthetic, analog and real lunar data, and studies failure modes such as craters, rocks and extreme shading.
